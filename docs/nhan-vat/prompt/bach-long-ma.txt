=== PROMPT CHÍNH (toàn thân – Bạch Long Mã, ảnh mẫu THAM KHẢO cho Bộ Bút) ===
(Đính kèm ảnh assets/nhan-vat/but/thay-tro.png để Gemini bắt cùng nét với 4 thầy trò.)

A flat 2D vector chibi mascot sticker of the White Dragon Horse (Bạch Long Mã) from Journey to the West — the dragon prince who became the monk's loyal white horse — drawn in EXACTLY the same style as the four characters in the attached image: ultra-minimalist rounded blocks, thick uniform black outline, flat colors, one simple flat cel-shadow tone. Soft cream background (#F6F3EC).

PROPORTIONS & CONSTRUCTION (chibi quadruped):
- Side view of a chubby little horse standing on four legs, body facing right, head turned 3/4 toward the viewer on the right side.
- One BIG rounded head (as big as the heads of the four characters in the attached image), sitting DIRECTLY on the front-top of the body with NO neck — the head overlaps the chest; the mane hides the join.
- A plump horizontal bean-shaped body, about 1.6x as long as the head is wide.
- Four short, thick, stubby legs with flat rounded hooves; the two far-side legs slightly darker.
- Total height (ground to top of ears) about the same as the standing characters in the attached image.

LINE & COLOR:
- Thick, UNIFORM black outline (#0A0807). Flat solid colors only. NO gradients, NO soft shading, NO blur, NO glow, NO 3D.

FACE (cute, loyal, gentle):
- Pure white coat (#FAFAF7) with a very light grey-blue shadow tone (#DCE3EA).
- A rounded soft muzzle slightly wider than the forehead, two small nostril dots, small friendly smile.
- Happy closed crescent eyes ^^ (like the old version), or gentle round eyes with a tiny glint. Faint pink cheeks.
- Two small pointed horse ears with pink inner ear.

DRAGON TRAITS (what makes him Bạch Long Mã, not an ordinary horse):
- Two small, short, rounded GOLD dragon horns (#E6B23C) on the forehead between the ears, simple antler-like with one tiny branch each.
- A soft wavy mane in pale aqua / jade (#9ED8D0) shaped like rounded cloud curls, running from the top of the head down the back of the head.
- A matching fluffy pale aqua tail made of 2–3 rounded cloud curls.
- Optional: two tiny curled dragon whiskers on the muzzle (very short, simple).

TACK / OUTFIT:
- A dark navy saddle (#2C2E44) with a gold trim (#D3A85B) sitting on the back, with one gold girth strap down the side of the belly.
- A dark navy neckerchief (#2C2E44) tied around the front of the chest under the head, with a small gold cloud motif.
- A thin gold bridle across the muzzle.

Full body, centered, clean adorable 2D mascot sticker, same line weight and simplicity as the attached characters. Avoid: 3D render, gradients, realistic horse anatomy, long neck, scary dragon, scales everywhere, wings, rider.
