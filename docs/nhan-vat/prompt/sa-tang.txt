=== PROMPT CHÍNH (toàn thân – quan trọng nhất, 1 tấm này là đủ để vẽ) ===

A flat 2D vector chibi mascot sticker of Sha Wujing (Sa Tăng / Sa Ngộ Tịnh), the loyal, calm and sturdy river-ogre monk from Journey to the West — honest, down-to-earth and quietly strong — rendered in an ultra-minimalist rounded-block style against a soft cream background (#F6F3EC).

PROPORTIONS & CONSTRUCTION (shared channel frame — keep identical to the other mascots):
- One big, PERFECTLY ROUND head in 3/4 view, turned slightly to the right, sitting directly on the body with NO neck.
- Short, stubby "tombstone" body like a rounded-corner rectangular block that sits flat on the ground with NO feet.
- Absolutely NO arms and NO legs. The entire left side of the body is completely flat and smooth, with no arm, hand, or visible limbs.
- On the right side, there is only a single small ROUND hand (a simple skin-tone circle) floating a short gap away from the body, appearing only to hold his staff.

LINE & COLOR:
- Thick, UNIFORM black outline of even, heavy weight everywhere (#0A0807).
- Flat solid colors only. NO gradients, NO soft shading, NO blur, NO glow, and NO 3D rendering.

FACE:
- Weathered tan / olive skin (#9C7B5A). A calm, honest, down-to-earth face: gentle oval eyes with a tiny bright glint, thick straight brows, a short tidy reddish-brown beard framing the jaw (simplified, rounded, NOT wild), faint cheeks, and a small calm closed-mouth smile. Loyal and steady expression, a little stern but kind. No wrinkles.
- Two plain rounded ears, one on each side, small and symmetric.

HAIR / HEAD:
- A shock of dark red-brown hair (#7A3B2C), short and neatly rounded (NO spikes, no tall crown). A thin simple gold forehead band may sit across the brow.

SIGNATURE NECK ITEM:
- His iconic rosary of nine skull beads around the neck — stylized SUPER CUTE and smooth, cream / ivory beads (#ECE3D0) with simple tiny dot eyes, friendly and rounded, absolutely NOT scary, gory or detailed.

OUTFIT:
- An earth-brown monk robe (#6E5438) as the base with a darker brown trim, simple and sturdy. A cream / ivory V-collar at the chest and a cream waist sash tied with a small round knot at the center of the blocky body. Optional thin gold trim. Humble traveling-monk look.

WEAPON / ITEM:
- He holds his "monster-taming" treasure staff (降妖宝杖 / Hàng yêu bảo trượng) in his single floating right hand: a tall straight bronze-gold pole (#C9A24A) standing upright close to the body, topped with a bright SILVER crescent-moon blade (nguyệt nha / lưỡi liềm trăng) and a small finial. The staff points up and away from the face.

Full body, centered, clean adorable 2D mascot sticker style with heavy outlines and a flat, boxy, super-simplified look, entirely without a left arm. Avoid: 3D render, gradients, shading, realistic details, scary skulls, grey robe, or any left arm.
